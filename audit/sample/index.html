Real self-audit sample · October 16, 2026, Central Time

A clearer purchase journey for Tony Intelligence.

This is a review of our own website before the corrections in this release. It is not a customer testimonial or proof of sales results.

Scope: homepage, intake, completion page, and sitemap. Public page text and source inspected. No private customer information was used. Direct ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI answer tests were not performed.

What is already working

The homepage identifies the $49 service, links to Stripe, explains the public-information approach, and gives a support email. Customers do not need to grant account access.

DO NOW · VERIFIED FINDING

Receipt language appeared without a verified transaction.

Observed: Opening the public intake page directly displayed a payment-confirmed message. The static completion page also asserted receipt of details without checking a submitted form.

Reasonable inference: Someone arriving directly could mistake a page visit for a completed purchase or submission. No lost purchases or actual confusion were measured.

Recommendation: Use conditional instructions. Confirm payment in Stripe and match usable intake before starting fulfillment.

Effort: small copy edit. Status: corrected in this release. Sources: intake and completion; pre-change source linked below.

DO NEXT · VERIFIED FINDING

The sitemap and indexing instruction disagreed.

Observed: The sitemap listed /audit-intake/, while that page included a noindex directive.

Reasonable inference: This gives inconsistent guidance about which pages should appear in search. It does not establish a penalty or ranking loss.

Recommendation: Keep customer utility pages accessible but out of the public sitemap; list the public offer page instead.

Effort: small configuration edit. Status: corrected in this release. Sources: sitemap and intake source.

LATER · RECOMMENDATION

Measure real customer response to the focused offer.

Observed: The homepage presented several different products. A dedicated audit page was not linked in the inspected navigation.

Reasonable inference: A focused page may make a targeted outreach message easier to understand. The effect is unmeasured.

Recommendation: Link outreach to one audit page and record genuine purchases separately from owner tests. Improve the offer only when objections and purchase data justify it.

Effort: small initial setup, then manual tracking. Status: focused page prepared; commercial outcome unknown.

Your next three actions

  1. Remove unverified receipt claims from public pages.
  2. Remove noindex intake URLs from the sitemap.
  3. Run a measured, approved customer test using the focused offer page.
