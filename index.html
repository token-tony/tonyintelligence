Guided business control

ChangeCheck

“A vendor asks you to change where future payments are sent. What happens next?”

A request like that may be completely legitimate.

It is also the kind of routine business process attackers try to exploit.

ChangeCheck gives the person handling the request a structured process to slow down, document what they checked, and decide what should happen next.

A longtime vendor emails

“We’ve changed banks. Please use the new payment information for future invoices.”

Instead of immediately changing the vendor record, ChangeCheck walks the employee through questions such as:

  • How was the request received?
  • Was the request expected?
  • Was the vendor contacted independently using previously trusted contact information?
  • Who performed that contact?
  • Were there inconsistencies or warning signs?
  • Does another person need to review the change?

ChangeCheck then creates a Payment Change Decision Record with one of three outcomes:

HOLDESCALATEPROCEED WITH INTERNAL UPDATE

What ChangeCheck does—and does not do

ChangeCheck helps document a human payment-change control process. It does not verify bank-account ownership, establish vendor identity, detect every fraud attempt, or guarantee that a payment is safe.

Educational contract support

ClauseGuard

“Before I click Accept—is there anything in these terms I should slow down and look at?”

Small businesses often adopt software without someone reviewing every commercial term.

ClauseGuard is a browser-local first-pass aid for supported AI, SaaS, and vendor agreement text. It helps surface terms that may deserve a closer look before a business signs, renews, or adopts a vendor.

Terms worth bringing forward

An agreement may contain:

  • automatic 12-month renewal
  • 60-day cancellation notice
  • unilateral price increases
  • broad rights to uploaded content
  • limitations on vendor liability
  • early termination restrictions

ClauseGuard brings those terms forward and explains why they may matter.

ClauseGuard provides educational first-pass support. It is not legal advice or a complete contract review, and it does not determine whether an agreement is legally safe or advisable.
